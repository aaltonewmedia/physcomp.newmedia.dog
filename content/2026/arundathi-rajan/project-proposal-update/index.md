### Updates!

Update: (19th November 2026) 

I've stumbled upon a term, "sousveillance" that is defined as the ***observation or recording from a human or bottom-up perspective** often using personal devices like smartphones, wearable cameras, or dashcams rather than from above by authorities or fixed infrastructure.* Coined by Steve Mann, the name is derived from the French word *sous* ("below") and *veiller* ("to watch"), contrasting with *surveillance* (*sur*, meaning "above").

There seems to be an interesting body of work, around this thematic, and attached below are a few links that given me some sense of inspiration.   
  
1. [https://www.domesticstreamers.com/work/recalibrating-the-panopticon/](https://www.domesticstreamers.com/work/recalibrating-the-panopticon/)
