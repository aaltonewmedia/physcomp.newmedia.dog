# **Project proposal: The Self-shredding Applicant**



My proposed project is an installation consisting of a receipt printer and a self-built paper shredder. The receipt printer continuously prints out applications that are fed directly into the shredder. This serves as a commentary on the often demoralizing application process to jobs, grants and subsidies. 

The applications are pre-written, possibly with some randomized elements but no AI.

###   
**Background**

Today, many avenues of life are exhaustingly competitive for young people. The job market is perhaps the clearest example. It is common to send tens if not hundreds of applications and be faced with rejection over and over again, usually without a further explanation. I think these circumstances make it very difficult for a person, especially someone young, to protect their sense of self-worth. 

Ironically, the job search can destroy what it necessitates; it is very difficult to maintain persistent effort if your self-efficacy is wavering. At its worst, this can lead to a person shooting themselves down before anyone else can. Hence, the Self-shredding Applicant.

###   
**Required materials**

- thermal receipt printer
- microcontroller
- power supply
- 2(?) motors for the paper shredder
- either recycled paper shredder blades or a DIY solution made with circular pieces of sharpened sheet metal and lasercut plywood
- words of advice 😅 (don't know how to do some things yet)
