In my artistic practice, I’m particularly interested in the themes of connection, empathy, and the implementation of therapeutic practices. One of the concepts I’ve been researching lately is *kinesthetic empathy* used in Dance/Movement Therapy (DMT). 

> *Kinesthetic empathy can be viewed and understood as the embodied experience of participatory knowing, the felt sense of reciprocity, the somatic resonance vibrating between and within the bodies, and establishing connections between bodies (Burns, 2011).*

In our daily life, kinesthetic empathy can be seen and experienced on the dance floor through the non-verbal physical attunement dancers experience when moving together. This experience allows individuals to sense and share the energy, rhythm, and emotional states of others without speaking. I find this experience really beautiful and inspiring. For this project, I wanted to translate this experience into something more intimate that can be shared between two people. 

So, my idea is to make a two-person audiovisual touch and heartbeat instrument. It acts as a sort of DIY synth, with each person interacting with a touch surface. Their bodies become part of the instrument. 

The heart rate sensor here acts as a secondary element (since it can be challenging to get an accurate reading instantly), influencing the musical qualities. There are several ways I could go about it, but for example, if players’ heart rates become closer, the sound could become more harmonious. The main idea is that it’s not just a separate input coming from each person, but their bodies are affecting each other’s sonic environment, which connects to the concept of kinesthetic empathy I’ve been so excited about. 

![](/images/2026/iuliana-fadeeva/mwhzno58-r8239dye.jpg)

In addition to the sound I want to connect it to the visual output using TouchDesigner to create audio reactive images creating a full audiovisual interactive experience.   
  
What I need for the project:   
  
Components   
- MPR121 capacitive touch sensor   
-  Pulse sensor   
-  Copper tape (touch pads)  
  
Software  
-  Max/MSP or PureData  
- TouchDesigner
