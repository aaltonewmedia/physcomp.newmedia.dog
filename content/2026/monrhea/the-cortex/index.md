A wearable performance instrument in the form of a hat, dubbed: The [CORTEX] (to symbolize the central node of operations), designed for live manipulation of sound and visuals through sensor-based interactions.

- I will incorporate an accelerometer to detect head movement and orientation, alongside capacitive touch sensors to detect touch interactions. The hat will be lined with programmable LED strips for visual enhancement.
- The hat will also function as a controller for manipulating visuals and audio in p5.js, translating physical movements and touch into real-time responses.

My inspiration was solidified by a tutorial on ADA fruits website - Build the Hat, a sound reactive LED hat. 

- [Build A Hat link](https://learn.adafruit.com/sound-reactive-led-top-hat/build-the-hat)
- [Demonstration Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CaQMgR3nY4Y)

I have been ideating this from the Computational Art and Design class. 

Find the documentation page [here](https://app.notion.com/p/MONRHEA-s-Wearable-Hat-V5-40850833cf5e80f2bf55fb96d74c0c94), as well as the notes on the progress. 

**Components and software's**

- Accelerometer MSA 311 sensor
- MPR121 Capacitive Touch Sensor (Optional)
- Visuals in p5.Js
- Sound in p5.Js and/or Ableton
- Microcontroller Raspberry Pi Pico 2W
- Rechargeable/replaceable battery?
- Plastic or Acrylic
